Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Maidurday Cleaning Service ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect information we receive from visitors and customers of our website and services.

1. Information We Collect

Account and booking information: Name, email address, phone number, service address, and payment method details (processed securely by Stripe — card data never touches our servers).

Usage data: Standard web server logs (IP address, browser type, pages visited) used for security and analytics. We do not sell this data.

Communications: Messages you send us via the contact form or email.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To schedule, manage, and confirm cleaning services
  • To process payments and send invoices and receipts
  • To send service reminders and notifications (you can opt out at any time)
  • To respond to inquiries and provide customer support
  • To improve our website and services

3. Payment Processing

Payments are processed by Stripe, a PCI-compliant payment processor. We store a reference to your payment transaction but never your card number, CVV, or full billing details. Stripe's privacy policy applies to data you provide during checkout.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share data with trusted service providers (e.g., Stripe for payments, email providers for transactional notifications) solely to operate our business. We will disclose data if required by law.

5. Data Retention

We retain your account and booking data for as long as you have an active account and for up to 7 years after your last transaction for legal and tax purposes.

6. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us at privacy@maidurday.com. We will respond within 30 days.

7. Cookies

We use session cookies required for authentication. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies. You can disable cookies in your browser, which may affect functionality.

8. Security

We use HTTPS, server-side security controls, and industry-standard practices to protect your data. No method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email us at privacy@maidurday.com or use our contact form.
