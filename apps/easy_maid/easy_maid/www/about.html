About Maidurday

Professional cleaning rooted in trust, reliability, and care for your home.

Our Story

Maidurday Cleaning Service was founded with a simple belief: everyone deserves a clean, comfortable home — without the stress of doing it themselves. Based in New Jersey, we combine professional-grade cleaning standards with the warmth of a local, family-run business.

We serve residential clients from single apartments to large family homes, as well as small commercial spaces. Every cleaner on our team is background-checked, trained to our standards, and genuinely cares about the results.

Our Values

🔒 Trust & Safety

Background-checked cleaners, insured service, and secure online payments.

✨ Quality First

We follow a consistent checklist on every visit so nothing gets missed.

🤝 Transparency

Upfront pricing, no hidden fees, and clear policies on cancellations and rescheduling.

🌿 Eco-Friendly

We offer green cleaning options using eco-safe products on request.

Our Team

Our cleaners live and work in the communities they serve. Each one is hired carefully, trained thoroughly, and supported so they can do their best work in your home. When you book with Maidurday, you get a consistent assigned cleaner who learns your preferences over time.
