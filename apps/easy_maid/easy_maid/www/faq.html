Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about booking and working with Maidurday.

How do I book a cleaning?

You can book online 24/7 at maidurday.com/book. Create a free account, choose your service and date, and pay securely via Stripe. You'll get an instant confirmation email.

What's included in a Standard Clean?

Standard Clean covers kitchen (countertops, sink, stovetop, microwave exterior, cabinet fronts), bathrooms (toilet, sink, shower/tub, mirrors, floors), and all living areas and bedrooms (dusting, vacuuming, mopping, trash removal). See our Services page for the full checklist.

What's the difference between a Standard and a Deep Clean?

A Deep Clean includes everything in a Standard Clean plus inside appliances (oven, microwave), baseboards and window sills, inside cabinets and drawers, and hard-to-reach spots. It's ideal for first-time clients or a thorough seasonal refresh.

How do recurring plans work?

Select weekly, biweekly, or monthly frequency when booking. You'll get a discounted rate and a consistent assigned cleaner. Each visit is automatically invoiced. You can cancel any individual visit with at least 24 hours' notice through your client portal — no penalty.

What is the cancellation / reschedule policy?

We require at least 24 hours' notice to cancel or reschedule a visit. You can do this anytime from your client portal. Cancellations inside the 24-hour window cannot be processed self-service; please contact us directly and we'll do our best to accommodate.

How do I pay? Is it secure?

All payments are processed securely via Stripe hosted checkout. Your card details are handled directly by Stripe — they never touch our servers. After completing a clean, you can view and pay invoices from your client portal anytime.

Do I have to be home during the cleaning?

No. Many clients provide a key code or leave a key. Our cleaners are background-checked and insured. You can also use your client portal to track visit status in real time.

Are your cleaners background-checked?

Yes. Every cleaner on the Maidurday team undergoes a thorough background check before their first assignment. They are also trained and insured.

Do you bring your own supplies?

Yes. Our cleaners bring all necessary cleaning products and equipment. If you prefer eco-friendly or specific products, let us know in the booking notes.

What areas do you serve?

We serve northern and central New Jersey including Essex, Hudson, Bergen, Union, Middlesex, and Morris counties. See our Service Areas page for the full list of towns.

Do you offer commercial cleaning?

Yes, for small commercial spaces. Request a quote with your square footage and requirements and we'll get back to you with a custom estimate.

How do I get in touch if I have a problem?

Email us at hello@maidurday.com, use the contact form, or call (555) 555-0123 during service hours (Mon–Sat, 8am–6pm).
